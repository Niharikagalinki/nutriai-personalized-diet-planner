ABOUT NUTRIAI

Nutrition Made Simpler

NutriAI is a student-built web application that creates personalized nutrition recommendations based on user goals, activity level, dietary preference, BMI and food preferences.

🎯

Personalized Planning

The application uses the information provided by the user to generate a customized nutrition plan.

📊

Health Insights

BMI, calorie requirements and macronutrient values are calculated and presented in a simple dashboard.

🥗

Meal Recommendations

Users receive breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner recommendations based on their preferences.
